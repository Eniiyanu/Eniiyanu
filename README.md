## 👋 About

I'm a software and ML engineer based in Nigeria, working globally and remotely. I build AI systems that are accessible to the people who need them, especially in languages and communities that mainstream tools overlook. My work spans multimodal learning, medical imaging, low-resource NLP and applied ML.

- 🧠 **AI/ML trainee at RAIN** (since May 2025), building multimodal agents
- 🗣️ **Founder & CTO of Kaanta AI**, which makes policy and compliance information available in Yorùbá, Igbo, Hausa and Pidgin
- 🔬 **Researching AfriCookBench**, a multimodal benchmark for guiding people through African cooking, starting with a Nigerian pilot in English and Yoruba
- 🎓 B.Eng. Information & Communication Engineering, FUTA (expected Aug 2026)
